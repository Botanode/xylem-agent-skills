# Benchmark Statistics

## When to use

Compute honest performance comparisons from admissible raw samples.

## Procedure

1. Use consistent units and ratio orientation; inspect distributions, noise, sample independence and measurement resolution.
2. Report sample counts, medians and suitable uncertainty; compute geometric means only across explicitly eligible positive ratios with consistent weighting.
3. Do not mix different campaigns, aggregate void results or conceal tails; label exploratory/outlier policies and limitations.

## Exit evidence

Recomputable summary with raw sample links, denominators, uncertainty and clearly stated exclusions.

## Guardrails

Apply the repository rules and current mission authorization. Do not invent source facts, bypass security controls, or claim checks that were not run. If verification is unavailable, mark the result pending or blocked with the exact reason and continue independent in-scope work.
